# Usamas Taxi Company · Lahore — AI Voice Dispatcher Demo (~3 min)

**Live:** https://taxi-dispatcher.usama-dispatch.workers.dev · **Bookings:** /bookings
*Before you start: use Chrome, allow the microphone, turn the volume up, press **↺ New call**.*

## 1. Intro (presenter, ~30s)
> "This is the AI dispatcher for Usamas Taxi Company in Lahore. Customers just talk to it, in English or Urdu, and it takes the whole booking: name, number, pickup, drop-off, time and passengers. Then it saves the booking straight into our system. Watch the orb: it reacts to my voice, spins while it thinks, and pulses when it speaks. The card on the right fills in as it hears each detail."

## 2. English call (tap 🎤, speak, tap again to send)
| # | Caller says | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Hi, my name is Ahmed Khan, my number is 0300 1234567." | Orb reacts to your voice → thinking ring → spoken reply asking for the pickup. **Name** and **Phone** fill in. |
| 2 | "Pick me up from Liberty Market and take me to Allama Iqbal Airport." | **Pickup** and **Drop-off** fill in; it asks for the time and passengers. |
| 3 | "Tomorrow at 9 am, two passengers, we have one big suitcase." | Time, passengers and notes fill in; it reads the whole booking back and asks you to confirm. |
| 4 | "Yes, that's correct." | Confetti and a ✅ **Booking confirmed** card with a reference number, e.g. **#5**. It also says the reference out loud. |

## 3. Roman Urdu call (press **New call** first)
| # | Caller says | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Assalam o alaikum, mera naam Bilal hai, number 0321 7654321." | Replies in Roman Urdu ("Wa alaikum salam Bilal sahab…") and asks for the pickup. |
| 2 | "Gulberg se Data Darbar jana hai." | Pickup and drop-off fill in; it asks for the time and how many people. |
| 3 | "Aaj shaam 6 baje, ek sawari." | Reads back all the details and asks "kya yeh sahi hai?" |
| 4 | "Haan bilkul theek hai." | Confirmation animation; "Aapka booking number … hai." |

*Tip: if the room is noisy, type the same lines in the text box. It works exactly the same way.*

## 4. Closing (~20s)
Click **Bookings →**. Both calls are there as rows: name, phone, pickup, drop-off, time, passengers and status *confirmed*.
> "That's a full taxi booking, in English or Urdu, with no one on the phone. Next step: connect a real phone number so customers can call it directly."
